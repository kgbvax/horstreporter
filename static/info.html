Horstreporter

Horstreporter's goal is to make current band-openings easy to explore (for DL9ET at least)
It is an alternate frontend for pskreporter.

Usage

Enter a locator (e.g., JO, FN31, JO32WE) or a callsign (e.g., W1AW) into the target box and click Go.

HorstReporter will fetch recent and live FT8/FT4 reports where the target is either the sender or receiver.

  • Filters: Select specific bands or apply minimum SNR thresholds (SSB/CW) to filter the visible spots.
  • Styles: Change the map visualization style (e.g., Grid SNR, Grid Age) using the dropdown.
  • Details: Hover over colored areas on the map to see top reports and signal statistics.

You can also change perspective by simply clicking on the map.

As with everything ham radio, this should be considered experimental and may implode any moment.
